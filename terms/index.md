# Kerja — Terms of use

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

## 1. About Kerja

Kerja is a professional networking app for digital nomads, starting in Canggu, Bali. It helps you find people to work with: at cafés, in work sessions, through profiles and through connection requests. It is not a dating app.

Kerja is run by Zayd Benhacine, an independent developer ("Kerja", "we"). Contact us at hello@trykerja.com.

Kerja is free to use. Kerja Plus, an optional subscription, adds more (section 9). We keep improving Kerja, so features may be added, changed or removed. These terms apply from the moment you accept them in the app.

## 2. Who can use Kerja

- You must be 18 or older.
- One account per person. Use your real name and describe your real work.
- Keep your sign-in to yourself. You're responsible for what happens on your account.
- Check in only at a café or other place you're actually at.

## 3. The rules: zero tolerance

Kerja has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive members. Don't post, send or do any of the following on your profile, in a connection request or its note, in a message or a table's chat, in an ask, in a session you host or an invite you send, in a place you suggest, or when you meet members:

- Harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation.
- Hate: attacking people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age.
- Sexual content or sexual advances. Kerja is for work: no dating, no hookups.
- Recruiting people into adult content through Kerja.
- Posting or linking explicit content. Your portfolio link can't point to an adult-content platform.
- Spam and selling: copy-paste or mass requests or invites, unwanted promotion, or selling to people who didn't ask. Asks can't include prices, tickets or selling.
- Scams and fraud: fake jobs or clients, asking for money, passwords or sign-in codes, investment schemes that promise easy returns, illegal gambling.
- Pretending to be someone else, or lying about who you work for.
- Sharing anyone's private details or photos without their consent.
- Unsafe behaviour at cafés or anywhere you meet through Kerja.
- Anything illegal where you are.

Breaking any of these rules ends in a ban.

## 4. How we enforce the rules

- A person reviews every report within 24 hours.
- We may remove content and ban accounts without notice when they break these terms or put members at risk.
- A session's host can remove a message or a member from its table's chat.
- A ban outlasts the account. We keep scrambled forms of a banned account's email addresses and Apple or Google sign-in IDs, so they can't be used to sign up again.
- Kerja automatically refuses some words. Getting past that filter doesn't make content acceptable.
- If you think we got a decision wrong, write to hello@trykerja.com.

## 5. Your content

What you put on Kerja (your profile, photo, notes, asks, sessions, messages and places you suggest) stays yours. You allow us to store it and show it to other members, as the privacy policy describes, only to run Kerja. When a place you suggest is added, its name and pin become part of Kerja's map. You must have the right to share what you post, and it must be honest.

## 6. Meeting in person

Kerja helps members meet at cafés and other public places. We don't check who members are, what they do, or whether an Instagram account is really theirs. Meet in public places, tell someone where you're going, and trust your judgment.

Work, prices and payments agreed between members are between you and them. Kerja isn't part of those agreements.

In an emergency, call the local emergency services (112 in Indonesia).

## 7. Report and block

On any member's profile, and in any chat, the ••• menu has Report and Block. You can also report a single message by pressing and holding it. Blocking takes effect at once: you and that person see nothing of each other. Report anything that breaks these terms or worries you.

Safety contact: hello@trykerja.com.

## 8. Leaving Kerja

You can delete your account at any time: Me → Settings → Delete account. If you signed in with Apple, the app asks you to sign in with Apple once more, so that we can also withdraw Kerja's access to your Apple Account. If you signed in with Google, we withdraw Kerja's access to your Google account. Your account and profile are removed as the privacy policy describes. Two things are kept:

- reports, for one year after we've reviewed them, even if either account is deleted;
- after a ban, the scrambled identifiers described in section 4.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel Kerja Plus: cancel it in your Apple Account settings first.

## 9. Kerja Plus

Kerja Plus is an optional subscription. It adds up to 10 intros a day, private sessions, meetings and events, sessions at any public place on Kerja's map, and one Boost a week. Joining sessions, hosting work sessions at cafés and saying hi stay free. Nobody sees who is on Plus.

- **Price and billing.** Kerja Plus is monthly or yearly, at the price the app shows before you buy, in your currency. It's bought and billed through your Apple Account, under Apple's terms.
- **Free trial.** A new subscriber can have a 14-day free trial, once per Apple Account. When it ends, the price is charged unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
- **Renewal and cancelling.** Kerja Plus renews automatically for the same period unless you turn off renewal at least 24 hours before the current period ends. If the price changes, Apple tells you before it applies. To cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or Manage in Apple Account in Kerja's Settings. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.
- **Deleting your account** doesn't cancel Kerja Plus. Apple keeps billing until you cancel it.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple: ask Apple for one.
- **Boost.** A boosted session comes first in Today and in People's Nearby until it starts, marked "Boosted". A boost made while you had Plus stays until its session starts.
- **Invite a friend.** When a friend signs up through your link and first checks in at a session someone else hosts, you each get one month of Kerja Plus, as one of Apple's one-time offer codes. It counts for each friend who joins, and a friend can be invited by one member only. Offer codes are redeemed in the App Store and have an expiry date. Inviting yourself, or making accounts to earn months, breaks section 2's one-account rule.
- **Founding hosts.** In Kerja's first weeks, members staying a month or more can apply to be one of up to 10 founding hosts, chosen by Kerja. A founding host gets a year of Kerja Plus when chosen, as one of Apple's offer codes, and hosts every week for 8 weeks.
- **Changes.** If we take a Kerja Plus feature away, we'll say so in the app first, and you can cancel. Nothing here limits your rights under your country's consumer law.
